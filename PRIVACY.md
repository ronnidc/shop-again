# Privacy policy for Shop Again?

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Shop Again? is a browser extension that keeps your own notes on web shops and shows them when you come back to a shop. This page says what it does with data. The short version: everything stays in your browser, and nothing is sent to anyone.

## What the extension stores

- The notes you write: the shop's domain, a verdict, the reasons you picked, an optional note in your own words, and a date.
- Your settings: how site access is asked for, and whether the banner tab and the right-click menu are on.
- For the current browser session only: which banners you have already seen, and a note you started but have not saved yet.

## Where it is stored

Notes and settings are kept in the browser's extension sync storage. If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, the browser copies them between your devices under your browser account, under the browser vendor's own privacy terms (Google for Chrome, Microsoft for Edge, Mozilla for Firefox). If not, they stay on this computer. Session data stays in memory and is gone when the browser closes.

## What the extension sends

Nothing. The extension has no server and makes no network requests. The developer never receives your notes, your browsing, or anything else. There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.

## What the extension reads

- The address of the page in the active tab, when you open the toolbar popup, to find out which shop you are on.
- The address of the page or of the link you right-clicked, when you use the right-click menu.
- Nothing else on the pages you visit. The banner script runs only on shops you have notes on and have given access to, and it only inserts the banner.

## Site access

No site access is requested when the extension is installed. Saving a note about a shop asks for access to that shop's domain, which is what lets the banner appear there. In the settings you can instead give access to every site once, or choose never to be asked, in which case there is no banner. Access to a shop is given back when its last note is deleted, and you can remove it at any time in the browser's own extension settings.

## Deleting your data

Delete a note or a shop in the extension, or remove the extension, and the data is gone from the browser's storage. If sync is on, the browser removes it from your other devices too. Exported files are yours and stay where you saved them.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
